Cameron’s sound world can be described in a myriad ways, but one that seems to fit best in regards to his dex recordings is in terms of dialectics. His performances are dazzling, combining disparate ends together to make one complete form. Think you’ve heard what a dulcimer sounds like? Think again. Seen everything there is to see with piano performance? Look again. Cameron’s output is both inspiring and striking (pun intended), with new, explorative methodologies toward instrument construction, preparation, and performance. While the performances are experimental – and there is a definite air of the avant-garde at play here – the sounds he creates have a wide variety of perspectives, benefits, and uses.
