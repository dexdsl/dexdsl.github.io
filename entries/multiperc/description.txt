As a percussionist, Ben Giroux's goal is to create unique sounds that you may have never heard before. Using a variety of instruments and objects from the classic snare drum and marimba to unconventional items like wine glasses and metal pipes, he aims to transport you into a whole new worlds of sound. Currently, Ben is furthering his education and skills by pursuing a Master of Music degree in Percussion Performance while continuing to practice and refine his craft at the Peabody Institute of The Johns Hopkins University.
