Aidan Yeats is a composer, songwriter, and poet based in Los Angeles, California who explores themes of romance, human love, and nature throughout his work. His poetic voice is an interweaving of southern roots and a southern California upbringing, underlain by an ancestral evocation of his Irish background. Containing influences from americana, post-punk, pop, country and alternative music genres, his solo discography consists of two full length albums, Virgin Forever and Ogre Angel. Yeats is now composing music for film and working on a score for a feature set to be released in 2026. He studied under Eyvind Kang at California Institute of the Arts and graduated in 2025 with a Performer-Composer degree.
