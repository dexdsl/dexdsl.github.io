Music to me is like a vessel of my soul, it carries me to my utopia. I often draw inspiration from the sounds of nature, in which I feel my mind blending with the tide and the gentle whispers of the wind. I look for resonance with the elements around me and believe that everything around me has a soul, and by hearing their sound I can add them to my creation of art.

I focus on compositions, electronic music, and sonic experiments. I love the sensations of clashing industrial and nature, and flowing between improvisation and structural. I think that conflicts rather creates exceptional sounds. That conflict is what I’m seeking in my music production. I hope my listeners can hear the tear but also feel the unity in those contradicting but coordinating sounds.
