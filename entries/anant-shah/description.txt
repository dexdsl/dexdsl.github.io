Anant Shah is a Massachusetts born and LA based Music Producer and Audio Engineer. Focused on working within the intersection of emerging musicians, songwriters, directors, filmmakers and animators, Anant’s audio expertise spans many mediums. His work spans album production, audio post-production, and composing immersive scores for Film & TV. Among his clients include Singer-Songwriters Alexandra Tejada & Nancy-Bleu, producing some of their first recorded singles, as well as the debut album of Jazz Drummer and Composer Yoshi Morimoto. Anant has composed music featured on Adult Swim in the Fish Tank bumper, a collaboration with director Angus Oakes and Maggie McKelvey, as well as composed original scores for many short films both live action and animated. Currently Anant works as Faculty at CalArts as special faculty in the Digital Arts Minor, and regularly performs in the Los Angeles area.
