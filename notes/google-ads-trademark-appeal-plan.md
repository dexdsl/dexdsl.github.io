# Google Ads — "Dex Digital" Trademark Appeal Plan

**Status:** Ready to execute — **free path only**. USPTO registration deferred (no resources right now).
**Urgency:** Low. The ad that was removed was a DSA ad in `Dynamic Sign-up Group`, which we're retiring anyway. The real value of appealing is **protecting the right to use our own name ("Dex Digital Sample Library") in ad text across the account**, not saving that one ad.
**Last updated:** 2026-07-03

> Not legal advice — this is a self-help template to review and fill in. If the complainant escalates beyond Google's ad platform, consult an IP attorney.

---

## Situation
- A dynamic ad was removed under Google Ads' **trademark policy** over the term **"Dex Digital."**
- We believe the complaint is weak — reads like a later entrant asserting a name we've long used.
- Our brand: **"Dex Digital Sample Library"** and **"Dex"**, in the library/museum (free digital sample library) category.
- We are **not** registered with the USPTO yet, and can't fund a registration right now.

## Strategy
Google generally **won't adjudicate** a trademark dispute between two parties. They will reinstate if we show the term is **our own established brand name used to describe our own services** (own-brand / nominative use). So the appeal argues *ownership of our own name*, not that the other party is wrong — and leans on **free** evidence.

---

## Near-term actions (free — do these)

1. **File the in-platform appeal** (Version A below).
   - Google Ads → **Policy manager** (or open the disapproved ad) → **Appeal** next to the trademark disapproval → paste Version A → attach the free evidence.
   - Review ~3–5 business days; ad doesn't serve during review.
2. **If no Appeal link / denied:** [Trademarks policy page](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6118?hl=en) → **Contact us** → topic **"Disapproved ads and policy"** → submit Version B + evidence. Request a **second review** if denied.
3. **Check the complainant** — free [USPTO TESS search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) for "Dex Digital." If they have **no live registration**, say so in the appeal; a complaint with no valid mark behind it is weak.

## Evidence to attach (all free / already have)
- Screenshot showing **"Dex Digital Sample Library"** is our org name (homepage/footer/about).
- **Proof of longevity**: incorporation docs, 501(c)(3) determination letter, and an **early archived version of the site** via the Wayback Machine (establishes first use — free).
- First-use date/year for "Dex Digital Sample Library" and "Dex."

## Deferred (when resources allow)
- **Register "Dex Digital Sample Library" with the USPTO** (~$250–350/class, DIY). This is the permanent fix — it makes this and any future challenge go away. Prioritize once budget exists, since someone is already circling the name.

## Version A — in-platform appeal box (≤500 chars, ready to paste)

```
These ads promote Dex Digital Sample Library — our own nonprofit
organization and brand name. "Dex Digital" is our own name, used only to
identify our own free digital sample-library services (library/museum
category). We use no third party's trademark. We're aware of no valid,
live USPTO registration for a conflicting "Dex Digital" mark in our field.
This is legitimate use of our own established brand; please reinstate.
Documentation of ownership available on request.
```

name. Supporting documentation is attached / available on request.
